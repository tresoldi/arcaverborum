## Linguistic and source safeguards

The original-source manifest pins immutable resource bytes and applicable licences. New namespaces are staged completely outside intake and appended with the guarded new-namespace tool; no subset overwrites an active source. Reviewed concepts enter through hashed primary/supplement ledgers, with literal original sense/POS/context and frozen definitions. Unreviewed intake is not automatically admitted. RefLex meanings and original dictionary meanings enter unmapped; individual decisions govern acceptance. LexiRumah's original Concepticon claims, elicitation notes and source/profile conventions remain native evidence, rather than a blanket semantic certificate.

Chakali and Dagaare profiles encode reviewed original phonetic fields under their actual published conventions, not inferred headword pronunciation. Beja explicitly uses IPA but has conversion warnings: selected readings agree with the PDF, and predictable unprinted stress/tone is not invented. The finite profiles do not generalize to unknown compounds. Dagaare `saa`/`zoore` homographs require separate pronunciation scopes and remain outside the shared exact-spelling profiles. Canonical segmentation can omit stress while original stress remains in native evidence; this limitation is explicit for Tuatschin.

Goo's acute/circumflex/unmarked/grave spelling is interpreted using Aplonova2020 pp7–8 as extra-high/high/low/extra-low, encoded by double acute/acute/grave/double grave. These are ordinal source categories, not measured pitch. The profile excludes nasal-conditioned consonants, uncertain vowels and zero-aperture notation. The thirteen remaining written records keep their original source IDs, concepts and evidence; their primary/supplement role and reading metadata change explicitly. No related RefLex wrappers are counted as independent attestations.

The three primary changes are quality-led, without a raw core-count veto or source-name blanket inversion. The193 older GLED forms preserve their earlier native readings, mappings, scores, cognacy, bibliography and comments; no GLED cognate judgments transfer to the new originals. Additional information in an original source is evidence about that witness, not independent corroboration of derivative comparative lists.

A new source is not a new language. Gaa/Tiba, Gula Iro and Ginyanga/Anyanga identities were reviewed; the eight LexiRumah pilots reconcile exact canonical codes and source names (including Rikou/Ringgou and Waerana/Kolor). Tuatschin is explicitly a named dialect under `ine-sursilvan`, with no invented independent code or map centroid. Kullilli/Kalali remains within Bulloo River. Unreviewed missing-code/dialect/family leads are not counted.
