## Intake and quality decisions to retain

* Acquire/stage complete source editions with immutable hashes. Preserve original headword, sense, POS, alternatives, inflection, page/native ID, author, lect/date, spelling and separately supplied or derived reading. Never overwrite accepted intake with a subset.
* Candidate semantic matching can propose literal dictionary senses at scale, but admission still distinguishes an attested meaning from a translation-chain guess. Keep unmatched source senses intact rather than forcing a close concept. No generic “old”/“egg”/species-to-animal merger.
* Use source-specific orthographic profiles when actual rules permit them; keep nonrecoverable distinctions unresolved. Valid tokens, larger GLED counts and source-name ranking do not decide quality. Compare original information and preserve earlier evidence at justified primary changes.
* Publish measured intake rows, reviewed lexical records, mapped/supported slots, new canonical languages and named stages separately. Reconstructions, shared wrappers and morphological variants are not extra independent languages or witnesses.
* Etruscan/Elamite remain protected priorities for genuinely new authority. T01–T08 completed slices, Na payload challenges, CHIRILA public-use/edition holds and other old acquisition failures remain in HELD; no new promise is made merely by repeating them.
