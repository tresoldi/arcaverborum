## Original evidence actually located or acquired

**Charrúa:** acquired Bertolotti/Coll's institutional *Retrato lingüístico del Uruguay* PDF (162 pages), whose printed p101/PDF102 reproduces a Vilardebó folio and lexical citations. Also acquired da Rosa2013's source-critical study. The two nineteenth-century witnesses must remain separate: Silva's1841 record, partly recalling earlier contact, and the1842 Arias-household informant. Attribution, remembered speech, loans, editorial readings and source dates are not equally secure. [The source-critical study](https://www.scielo.cl/scielo.php?pid=S0718-93032013000200007&script=sci_arttext) expressly challenges treating the two vocabularies as one homogeneous corpus. The [institutional facsimile/context](https://udelar.edu.uy/eduper/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2015/09/retrato.pdf) makes a bounded documentary pilot feasible after edition/term review.

A community glossary at [Memoria Charrúa](https://memoriacharrua.uy/glosario-charrua/) is a useful comparison/modern-curation witness, not independent nineteenth-century confirmation. It states all rights reserved and combines words, phrases, uncertain glosses and ethnographic interpretation. Do not bulk-copy it as a licensed dictionary or interpret bracketed editorial forms in another source as original supplied IPA. The1969 Boletín source route identified in the investigation failed DNS resolution; no full1969 payload is claimed. Immediate target yield is unmeasured; written historical citations are a legitimate outcome without invented pronunciation.

**Chaná:** acquired the full334-page institutional ANÁFORAS scan of Larrañaga's *Escritos*, tomoIII. The available title page says1924; the work is often cited as1923. Preserve that edition discrepancy instead of silently changing it. The *Compendio* starts at PDF185, with the twelve-page body at printed pp163–174/PDF187–198 (title/blank pages PDF185–186); inspect exact printed-page/date boundaries during extraction. Historical Soriano material must stay distinct from the contemporary Blas Jaime/Viegas Barros2013 construction. The [modern Wiktionary appendix](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Chan%C3%A1_word_list) has a substantial Spanish/English/Chaná table, absent from our one-word main-entry intake; these are not automatically distinct lemmas or core senses. Establish its revision and attribution/underlying book terms, check original Spanish and the2013 phonology before deriving profiles. A generic Spanish spelling parser is inadequate. Original2013 book payload/redistribution terms are not established in this investigation.

**Güenoa/Minuán:** Hervás1787's catechism and its later analyses are an original-text acquisition route. This is a short text, not a dictionary; whole clauses cannot be mechanically divided into word/gloss alignments. Separate an ethnic label from an attested source-language identity. Minuán, Bohán, Yaro, Mbeguá and Timbú must not become additional accepted languages simply by copying ethnonym lists. No new Güenoa lexical payload is acquired in this pass.

**Laklãnõ/Xokleng:** acquired Gakran2015's284-page [original institutional grammar](https://repositorio.unb.br/bitstream/10482/19096/1/2015_Nambl%C3%A1Gakran.pdf). ChapterII explicitly treats phonemes and orthography; its contents list consonant/vowel correspondence tables at printed pp70–71. This is much stronger profile authority than simply resegmenting the current DIACL strings. It is not itself an acquired complete dictionary. The bibliographic Gakran2013 dictionary lead is catalogued separately; dictionary-scale yield and applicable reuse terms remain gates. Apply a profile only to matching original-source examples or demonstrably matching spelling conventions, not every historical Xokleng record.

**Patagonia:** the original [CHICO-SC project source inventory](https://chico-sc.ppls.ed.ac.uk/es/gunun-chonan/) provides original-source, consultant/date and archive leads for Günün a Yajüch, Tehuelche, Selk'nam, Haush and Teushen, including Haush manuscript leads. An inventory/archival description is not an acquired lexical payload or permission to redistribute it. “Günün-Chonan” is explicitly a proposed relationship on that site; keep Arca's existing Puelche/Chonan classifications unless separately reviewed evidence justifies changing them.

One parsed record has an incomplete field count. These are review/transport issues, not new-language totals. There are64 absent-exact-code member leads, including retired/unknown codes, dialects and represented aliases—not64 new languages. Example: the old “Diyari” member is coded Dirari; “Wemba-Wemba” is coded Western Victoria. Name/ISO/source identity review precedes any accepted linkage.

There are227,014 nonempty `PhonemicisedIPA` fields. They are upstream phonemicization, not automatically the cited author's supplied IPA. Preserve raw `OriginalForm`, `OriginalGloss`, original POS, standardized gloss, author, variety, identifiers and coordinates.11,167 rows have reconstruction attachments; retain these as explicitly separate hypotheses, never additional daughter attestations or automatically expert cognate assignments. There is no source-edition shortcode field in this export, despite the FAQ instructing users to cite one: author names such as “The Writer” cannot identify an edition reliably.

After normalizing the three header-name differences, the multiset comparison leaves6,194 unmatched browsing-row occurrences;6,192 have no identical full-export payload anywhere. The five-field member/form/original-gloss/converted-reading/author comparison leaves1,176 unmatched occurrences;1,117 have no identical tuple anywhere. The files may reflect different selection/editing states. Do not concatenate them as additive evidence or ingest one as a purported complete replacement of the other.

## CHIRILA terms and the2025 collection are separate gates

The archived [site footer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YaleDHLab/chirila/ef69c2727ff272f9ce3752cc2079fa8a819f3ac7/views/base.pug) identifies CC BY-NC-ND4.0; the [download conditions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YaleDHLab/chirila/ef69c2727ff272f9ce3752cc2079fa8a819f3ac7/views/download.pug) add noncommercial use, original-source/database acknowledgement, registration and error-reporting conditions. GitHub's repository metadata has no standalone licence assignment. That does not erase the site's data notice, nor does public code/data access imply unrestricted transformed redistribution.

The [CC legal text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode.en) distinguishes technical format modifications from adaptation and permits some noncommercial uses while restricting sharing adapted material; database rights also matter. Do not claim that every CSV conversion is forbidden, or assume that Arca's semantic mappings/profiles/combined database exports are automatically covered. Reconcile the applicable historical release terms and intended public output, or acquire the underlying original sources under suitable terms. No registration/contact is made here.

Fresh metadata for [Yale Dataverse2025](https://dataverse.yale.edu/dataverse/chirila), doi10.60600/YU/XHPJXB version1.0, still shows715 files/706 restricted. The nine public-flag files are metadata tables, not the roughly950,000 lexical items. An ordinary attempt to download `sources.tab` returned HTTP400 requiring a Guestbook response. No Guestbook form, access application, contact, workaround or restricted lexical download occurred. Public file flags alone do not establish anonymous download access.
