## Final quality corrections

[Three new readings were withheld before publication](worldwide_followup_final_pos_review_2026-10-29.csv):
Nen and Teanu boat-sail nouns cannot map to the verb sail; Kalamang drill verb
cannot map to the power-tool noun. Original rows remain in full raw intake.
They are excluded from the543 accepted additions. The finite-profile compiler
now rejects explicit dictionary noun/verb conflicts, including Nen's common-noun
labels; it does not certify all other POS or semantic relations automatically.

Coptic materialization now supports independently hashed source files and
resource-specific parameter IDs. Alpha's36 earlier rows/parameters remain exact;
Beta adds22 without overwriting earlier sense labels. Tests reject changed or
unpinned files. Exact registered source/target scopes also survive profile
compilation, preserving Sanzhi's attribution on reproduction.
