## PROJ-04: witness scopes and source-visible review

[146-case workspace](https://arcaverborum.org/review/linguistic-2026-11-02/index.html) presents
original records, supplied readings, native sense evidence and the two earlier
model reviews with disagreements. Supplemental native parameter labels are
visible, with an explicit unblinded label. The separate
[six-flag scope workspace](https://arcaverborum.org/review/scopes-2026-11-02/index.html) starts
from the six documented pooled-versus-publication flags. These are review tasks,
not six corrected words or six additional independent positive events.
[Scope packet metadata](project_scope_review_2026-11-02.json).

Scope assertions attach to exact original record IDs and distinguish field
collection, original publication, edition, local variety, historical stage and
reading convention. Publications/editions can incorporate or reproduce several
field collections; the dimensions cannot safely be reduced to a citation-string
partition. The diagnostic returns `matched_scope` only with complete matching
witness/variety/stage/reading evidence; a known disagreement is `different_scope`,
and missing evidence stays `unknown`. None means a negative language observation.
The diagnostic does not change production graph weights or infer independence.

The pages run without network services; decisions stay in the browser session
until exported. CSV/native text is rendered as text, not executable markup.
Exports bind packet and case hashes and require reviewer provenance, rationale
and an original-source locator. Validation rejects stale evidence, invented or
duplicate scoped records and unsupported provenance. Combined packet inputs are
capped at16MiB, decision files at8MiB. Even a human-labelled export does not
automatically constitute specialist gold. No adjudication is invented to close
this track.

Rebuild with `scripts/build_review_workspace.py`; pass `--review` for each model
CSV and `--native-labels` for the supplement. Validate an export with the same
inputs plus `--decisions path.json`. Original sealed worksheets and analytical
snapshots remain private reproducible inputs. The public packet JSON can also be
downloaded alongside each page.
