## WN09 — Source fidelity and concept-level preference

The explorer and CSV/TSV exports now provide:

* `preferred_source`: configured primary only where it supplies a supported
  reading for that concept; blank otherwise, without guessing a winning alternative.
* `evidence_role` and `gled_dependence`: distinguish preferred support, supported
  alternatives, legacy overlap/residuals and concepts with GLED-only support.
* `representation_basis`: distinguish ASJP-derived material, supplied IPA,
  profiles, upstream segments/conversions and written/unresolved evidence.
* `phonological_marks`: observed quantity, nasalization, pitch/accent, stress and
  laryngeal/aspiration marks. Absence is not proof of absent contrasts or full tone.

Preference/dependence uses the whole accepted construction; the interface states
that collection filters may omit some sources. No fixed source-name ranking or
new confidence score is introduced. Existing scores remain feature/coverage
summaries, separate from source fidelity and independent corroboration.

`scripts/report_source_fidelity.py` generates concept-level CSV and a summary;
[summary](worldwide_wn_source_fidelity_2026-10-29.json) records measured dependence.
Additional preferred notation is a review lead, not a claim of lost phonemic
contrasts in identical words. New tests cover residual dependence, unsupported
readings, no implicit source ranking and descriptive notation marking.
