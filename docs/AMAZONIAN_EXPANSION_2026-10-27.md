## 4. Dictionary senses and Iquito profile

**Media Lengua:** 220 additional selected literal/bilingual mappings bring the
construction to **397 guarded decisions, 331 mapped concepts and 132 supported
core concepts** (previously 124). All 3,216 lexical/transcription records and all
previous mappings are preserved. Concrete vocabulary expands substantially
beyond the core. These are authored sense decisions, not a bulk policy equating
English strings. Ambiguous homonyms were not automatically admitted.

A few core mappings require bilingual disambiguation: the English gloss breath
is source-labelled a verb with Spanish respirar; formal singular you preserves
its register, and one retains its article polysemy. Source descriptions remain
unchanged. See the [dictionary-sense review](amazonian_expansion_dictionary_senses_2026-10-27.csv).

**Iquito:** 44 additional semantic decisions and a narrowly documented contextual
extension raise dictionary profile forms **26 → 69**, bringing the construction
to **425 forms**. Supported core remains **129/161**; the gains broaden lexical
coverage rather than inflating core counts. Of 106 reviewed senses, **69 are
admitted and 37 held**. Old-man and old-woman nouns map to their own concepts,
not the old adjective.

Michael and Beier (2021), Dictionaria 14, table 4 distinguishes the ni+vowel
consonant grapheme from short n+i before a consonant or word edge (ani /ani/).
The profile retains that distinction without inferring surface allophones.
Mixed-tone long vowels and remaining s/ʃ uncertainties remain held; low/null
tone is not guessed. All profile output remains explicitly derived and scoped
to the dictionary source. No primary-source records are affected.

The [current profile snapshot](amazonian_expansion_iquito_profile_2026-10-27.csv)
is separate from the unchanged first-pilot dated CSV. Regenerable reports now
default to ignored `output/report/`; use `--review-output` to save a reviewed
snapshot. Guarayu's unresolved nasal/glottal/dialect issues remain held.

## 5. Missing-language evidence: Bangru admitted from the original

No distribution license was established for the Wust derivative in this review,
and its empty segment fields are not promoted to supplied IPA. Instead, the original author deposit
provides a separate, explicit **CC BY 4.0** route:

* Timotheus Adrianus Bodt (2017), *Bangru Language Data - various descriptions
  and analyses*, doi:10.5281/zenodo.1116263.
* The original *Bangru Wordlist.pdf*, SHA256
  `61760ad451c9594d7d82bb04fa24b392c592cb88f0d153cd8a90e813c816a9fa`.
* The overview deposit, doi:10.5281/zenodo.1116298, identifies the speaker-A
  recording series and Pisa Chachung; fieldwork was in Sarli circle in 2013.
* The author's licensed NEILS 2014 phonology presentation,
  doi:10.5281/zenodo.1116254, slide 9 documents the affricates used for tokenization.

The first accepted `sit-bangru` construction contains **99 supplied-IPA forms
covering 95 core concepts**, from one consistently identified speaker. The
canonical language is bang1369; Sarli dialect leva1238 belongs to that language.
No earlier accepted sample was found under either identity. Reference map
coordinates come from Glottolog and are not claimed as the speaker's GPS point.

All **119 authored core-sense entry spans** were verified against the original
PDF; 95 entries are admitted and 24 held. Every alternative in an admitted A
span is retained, producing 99 forms. Source pages, complete entries, speaker
labels, raw transcriptions and hashes survive in comments. Comparative-language
values, tentative roots and reconstructions in the same document never become
Bangru forms or cognate judgments. No expert/computed cognacy is inferred.

Tokenization preserves the original IPA; tʃ/dʒ are tied according to the source's
phoneme inventory. Phonation, tone, length, nasalization and word boundaries are
retained. Optionality, unclear stress conventions, unsupported affricate-like
sequences, ambiguous spacing and insufficiently specific senses remain held.
The authored ledger is `src/arcaverborum/data/bangru_original_forms.csv`; its
source manifest records document and reference hashes separately.

`stage_bangru_original.py --fetch --audit-pdf` verifies the frozen document and
materializes a CSVW/CLDF wordlist with source references and column identities.
PyMuPDF is optional for the PDF-span audit (the reviewed extraction used 2.0).
Generic source fetching recognizes this document deposit instead of attempting
a Git clone. Only the staged **new namespace** was appended to active intake;
existing source tables were not subset-ingested or refreshed.

**Still held:** NLS's Belhariya and Chhulung entries lack reliable original
attribution. Related historical survey publications cannot be grafted onto
unlinked modern village rows. Chhulung member/location labels also require
reconciliation. Tangam's original monograph is identified, but a reviewed,
redistributable lexical slice remains unresolved; public recordings have
item-specific access conditions. Bangru's original license does not retroactively
license the derivative repository. See [the blocker follow-up](amazonian_expansion_missing_languages_2026-10-27.csv).
