## Recommendation

Make the next cycle predominantly lexical: deeper source-faithful coverage,
original-source diversity and native sense review. Continue colexification through
observation quality and adjudication, rather than another more elaborate model.
Restore equal attention to Arca's other purpose: a reliable substrate for cognator
and regulae. Deliver improvements as reviewed data and reproducible releases,
with the explorer making the evidence understandable.

Current accepted baseline:7,366 nonempty constructions,2,119,818 records,
470,116 supported core slots and1,552 GLED primaries. These are constructions,
not a count of independent living languages. CINF's326 spelling repairs,109
phonemic recoveries,76-pair experiment and model-assisted146-case reviews are
completed work; expert gold remains zero. No repeat of those completed cohorts
or blanket promotion of experimental confidence is proposed.

Fresh read-only reports found6,837 overlapping member-level additive leads,

## PROJ-01 — Restore trustworthy readings and deepen authoritative constructions

Start with `tuc-wa-ikhana` / `hubercolumbian_Piratapuyo`: accepted supported core57;
fresh upstream screen shows86 potentially additional slots. Compare the original
Huber–Reed1992 conventions, exact sample and senses with HGN137 and Chacon's
Piratapuyo derivative. Those related witnesses may share ancestry; do not count
three namespaces as three confirmations or sum86 and30. Wa’ikhana's123 restored
written-only forms are already accepted spelling evidence, not an absent import.

Then review remaining source evidence for quality-led authority changes in
Ampari Dogon (`heathdogon_Ampari`), Cayuga (`cayugaoriginal`) and Isthmus Zapotec
(`dictdiidxaza`/`dictdidxazageneral`). All still have GLED primaries and already
have finite reviewed alternatives; do not readmit their completed cohorts.
Ampari's raw73-versus51 supported-core comparison includes nine potential losses
and does not certify73 trustworthy readings. Original nasalization/compound rules,
Cayuga complete-word/possessor/whisper scope, and Zapotec tone/laryngealization
and thematic/lect breadth are the next gates. Richer trustworthy Wiktionary
members remain eligible on the same evidence basis; no fixed second-tier GLED rule.

Deliverable: guarded new reading/admission decisions, an explicit primary recipe
where justified, and a source-quality/gap comparison retaining earlier evidence.
Benefit: useful depth and lower ASJP dependence. Cost: source-specific linguistic
work; phonetic support can fall when earlier confidence was unfounded. No
promotion or numerical gain is promised by this plan.

## PROJ-02 — Original-source coverage, with an Americas-first global rotation

Begin with the already acquired Bove1883 Yagán facsimile: independently transcribe
and align a bounded lexical section against existing Yámana, preserving historical
witnesses and uncertainty. The222-page PDF is acquired; another download is not
a new achievement. Original-source transcription is the gate, not modern IPA.

Follow with one bounded northeastern Brazilian historical section from the
acquired Mamiani/Nantes/coastal corpus, distinguishing historical Kipeá,
Dzubukuá and editorial layers. Kipeá's16 written citations and the old mixed kzw
rows are existing evidence; do not relabel them wholesale or count ethnonyms as
new languages. Select a source-attributed African/RefLex cohort from the already
complete intake with real bibliography and reading authority; Bekwel2555 BIB=0
and mixed Ndebele/Kamba samples remain held. Full RefLex and CHIRILA transport
are completed, not proposed imports.

Maintain a rotation into Asian/Mongolic and New Guinea/Torricelli gaps using the
fresh missing-member/additive audit, exact language-level identity and populated
original lexical sources. Select those exact cohorts before execution; geography
alone does not supply a source. Etruscan `neri`/`phersu` and Elamite EW111/499/600
remain protected research lanes, reopened by new attributable lexical authority,
not repeated failed access attempts or conjectural readings.

Deliverable: source/page/entry ledgers and a bounded accepted cohort, or a precise
hold with zero gains. Benefit: fills neglected geographic and historical gaps.
Cost: slower than converted-list admission; some useful evidence stays written.
New languages, named varieties and depth in existing constructions are separate.

## PROJ-03 — Strengthen native senses and reviewed WordNet links

Only190 active concepts currently have any accepted WordNet link;141 links are
exact, while2,365 concepts have candidates but no link and1,202 only inherited
associations. Freeze a first review cohort of50 high-use or ambiguity-prone
concepts from accepted output before inspecting candidate outcomes. Improve
source-native sense/POS/context and lexeme-to-concept decisions alongside the
concept-level links; linking an English label cannot validate a source word.

Use complete dictionaries already in intake (including Pite, Cayuga and RefLex)
to retain more literal native senses and safely map exact meanings. Preserve
unmapped vocabulary and source lexical structure when the concept catalog is too
coarse. Keep OEWN2025/PWN3.0 crosswalks and exact/broader/narrower/related links
distinct. Function words, classifiers and culturally specific meanings may have
no appropriate English synset; do not force one or propagate WordNet links into
new lexical colexifications.

Deliverable: source-guarded sense decisions and50 documented concept reviews,
not a promised50 accepted exact mappings. Benefit: improves search, comparison
and semantic validity together. Cost: expert editorial work; raw breadth can
exceed defensible conceptual comparability.

## PROJ-04 — Observation scopes and a usable linguistic review workflow

Take the six documented pooled-versus-within-publication flags first, with the
146-case reviews and200 positive controls lacking comparable singleton citations.
Specify a typed hierarchy of edition, original publication/field collection,
local variety/stage and reading convention. A shared bibliography key does not
establish one field witness; splitting every citation string is equally unsafe.
Test scope changes on exact cases before a corpus-wide analytical refresh.

Build on the existing CSV review machinery with a local review workspace that
presents original forms, senses, notation and available source locators together,
records reviewer provenance/disagreements, and exports versioned guarded decisions.
Model assistance stays labelled. Human specialist judgment is a genuine remaining
gate; another pair of model reviews cannot substitute for it.

Deliverable: reviewed scope rules, regression cases and an adjudication packet
usable by linguists. Benefit: directly addresses aggregation and semantic errors.
Cost: original-witness access and specialist time. Defer a larger confidence model
until adequate positive-event power and real annotation evidence exist; the
sampled common/rare76-pair panels had only4/0 positives.

## PROJ-05 — Cognacy and morphological integrity for downstream users

Refresh the existing cognate-source audit before choosing a repair cohort; do not
repeat already implemented ABVD/URALEX scope repairs. Historical audit leads
include ACD, Sagart Sino-Tibetan and Lee Japonic raw/structured identifier
conventions, not established errors in every coded record. Check membership
namespaces and expert/computed status, within-meaning versus cross-meaning IDs,
full-form/stem/phrase distinctions, and whether alignments and morpheme/segment
slices still refer to the retained reading after a transcription change.

Keep colexification, homophony and cognacy separate. A shared source ID is not
an automatic cross-source bridge or proof of polysemy. Assemble small provenance-
rich fixtures from several families for cognator/regulae interoperability before
proposing a general inferred-cognate layer.

Deliverable: a measured anomaly queue, source-specific guarded repairs only where
proven, and downstream fixtures. Benefit: strengthens Arca beyond its semantic
explorer. Cost: source schemas differ; many apparent anomalies may be legitimate
local conventions, and no automatic cognate reconciliation is authorized.

## PROJ-06 — A citable whole-collection release and clearer evidence use

The historical Core0.1.0 release is distinct from the current All accepted
collection. Prepare a dated, reproducible whole-collection release with input
pins, authority/profile recipes, per-source license/attribution manifest, stable
IDs, explicit evidence classes and machine-readable changes. Verify reproducible
exports and failed-build/zero-record handling. Preserve known-family and original-
witness dependence qualifications in derived research exports.

Refine the existing provenance/search/export interface around concrete tasks:
why a reading/source was selected, what remains written-only or reduced, how to
inspect original senses, and how to reproduce a selection. Evidence-role fields,
concept-entry tables and maps already exist; audit their clarity rather than
reimplementing them or starting a cosmetic redesign. Add coverage/source-quality
change summaries as they become useful to reviewers.

The last measured site/docs footprint is about746MB, after complete obsolete
generations were retired;985MB was the earlier regional deployment. This is not
an immediate storage emergency. Plan versioned bulk-download storage and retain
active-reference/size checks before growth demands it; never publish a partial
catalog to fit a budget.

Deliverable: a reproducible release candidate and reviewed research-user flows.
Benefit: makes the accumulated data citable and usable outside the website.
Cost: release maintenance and evidence/format discipline; website counts alone
are not release validation.
