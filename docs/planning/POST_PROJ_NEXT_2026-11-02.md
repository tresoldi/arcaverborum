### NXT-01 — Repair catalog sense lineage before expanding its consequences

Baseline: six catalog label/definition conflicts expose573 lexical assignments
for review, not573 proven errors. BECAUSE OF accounts for49, BEAD516, four

### NXT-02 — Expand and audit WordNet in evidence-ranked batches

Baseline:1,123 of3,789 active concepts have accepted typed links;2,627 pending,
12 held,27 inventory gaps. First audit a stratified sample of the940 new links,
covering exact and directional approximate relations, POS and kinship/taxonomic
boundaries. A second model pass can expose disagreements but is not independent
expert validation. Then rank pending concepts by accepted lexical exposure and
research usefulness; review original definitions and candidate synset definitions
in bounded batches. Continue while defensible matches are available, without a
numerical acceptance quota. Inspect inventory gaps against the pinned OEWN data
before interpreting missing retrieval as missing ontology coverage.

Benefit: useful alignment at scale with measurable review provenance. Cost:
English WordNet cannot encode every culturally or grammatically specific concept.
Retain explicit no-match/hold decisions, Princeton compatibility and relation
direction; WordNet never creates lexical equivalence or colexification evidence.
Deliverable: expanded typed alignment plus a disagreement/error audit of the
previous batch, not a calibrated confidence claim.
