## Primary-source checks made for this proposal

* [Basa original dictionary](https://rogerblench.info/Language/Niger-Congo/BC/Kainji/Basa/Basa%20Dictionary%202019%20complete.pdf) identifies ruBasa/Basa-Benue in Nigeria and the Imoh/Hyslop collaboration. This helps distinguish accepted Basa Cameroon, but exact edition and RefLex attribution still need collation.
* [Mainz comparative Gəmme/Vere wordlists](https://www.blogs.uni-mainz.de/fb07-adamawa/files/2015/06/Gimme-Vere-Doyayo.pdf) distinguish Gəmnəme and Gəmme/Kompana subdivisions and named lects. Do not pool Gimme, Gimnime, Momi or Jango by similar names.
* [Iwaidja original introduction](https://ausil.org.au/Dictionary/Iwaidja/Iwaidja-intro.htm) identifies compilers, collection period, revised manuscript lineage and copyright. No new reuse permission is claimed.
* [Nen original guide](https://dictionaria.clld.org/contributions/nen) describes unwritten predictable schwa, accepted pronunciation alternatives and source-language predicate classes. [Wersing guide](https://dictionaria.clld.org/contributions/wersing) distinguishes headword forms, echo vowels and uttered variants. These are concrete profile/citation-form gates, not tokenization problems.
* [PARADISEC access workflow](https://paradisec-archive.github.io/PARADISEC_workflows/02_getting-started.html) requires registration/login for open-access collections; no access or redistribution right is inferred from an archive catalog.
