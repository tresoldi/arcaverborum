## Interpretation and verification

Written-only rows are explicitly guarded, retain blank Segments and an unsupported
status even for Latin-looking forms. They improve lexical/script/metadata coverage
without inflating supported phonetic counts. Historical reading metadata lives in
exported comments and participates in build freshness. Exact model/scope metadata
adds epistemic context; the existing `source` status still identifies segment
origin and is not a claim of direct ancient phonetic observation.

No existing row is intentionally removed or rewritten apart from additive
historical evidence in its comment. Existing source cognacy namespaces and primary
profiles are retained. The Elamite audit preserves original dictionary comments
and references, including pooled OE/ME/NE/Achaemenid notation, without assigning
an invented single language period.

Final semantic cross-check: Latin `filius` is mapped explicitly to SON, retaining
its original English CHILD gloss, rather than contributing a generic CHILD core
slot. The native entry distinguishes masculine singular son from plural children
([entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/filius#Latin), checked2026-10-28).
Sanskrit right-side `dakṣa`, walking `calati` and chewing `khādati` were also
checked against their native lexical senses; no figurative/metaphorical sense is
needed for those admissions. Qualified pronunciation scope remains the frozen
snapshot, rather than silently updating sound evidence from live pages.
