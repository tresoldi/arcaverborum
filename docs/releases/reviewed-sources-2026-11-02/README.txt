Arca Verborum — Reviewed sources 2026-11-02

A lexical subset of the accepted explorer, selected by reviewed source terms. It is not All accepted, the historical Core release, a new language census, or a complete original dictionary dump. Recording files and source scans are excluded.

Each lexical row retains all accepted fields plus release_source_license. Source licenses, contributors, snapshots, attribution and modifications are in licenses/source-reviews.json, data/metadata.csv, data/sources.bib and original row provenance. Keep these notices and the original per-entry URLs when redistributing. Wiktionary-derived records and Arca adaptations to those records are CC BY-SA 4.0; other source terms remain as declared per row, not replaced by the code MIT license.

Arca selects, normalizes and annotates source material and reviews semantic mappings. Source and profile flags include documented upstream conversions; they are not independent certification of IPA. Written-only and unresolved evidence remain explicit. Typed WordNet links, inherited associations and retrieval candidates are distinct. Source-specific cognacy does not imply independent fieldwork or colexification.

Cite Arca Verborum, Reviewed sources 2026-11-02, archive SHA256, together with the individual original sources used. No DOI or new version of the historical CITATION.cff is asserted.
