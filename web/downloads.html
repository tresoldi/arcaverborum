Interpreting the evidence

Supplied or upstream-converted readings are distinguished from Arca profiles, resegmented spelling and unresolved written evidence. These flags do not independently certify IPA. Literal source senses remain visible when no defensible catalog mapping exists. Source-specific cognacy is separate from colexification.

The concept catalog and WordNet sidecar include reviewed exact, broader, narrower and related links, alongside explicitly distinguished inherited associations and retrieval candidates. These links do not rewrite lexical meanings, and candidate scores are not calibrated confidence estimates.
