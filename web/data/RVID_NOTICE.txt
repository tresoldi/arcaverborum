Arca Verborum's rvidinfinitives derivative selects reviewed infinitives from
four source samples, tokenizes supplied IPA, maps reviewed modern meanings,
and preserves source lexical comments, cell labels, original IDs and bibliography.
It does not infer meanings from Latin etyma or treat inflected cells as lemmas.
This data component remains under GPLv3; see RVID_GPL-3.0.txt.
Other data components retain their own per-source terms. The repository code's
MIT license does not relicense the RVID data.
